About six weeks ago, Toni Schneider dropped the "interim" from his job title to take on the role of Bluesky CEO full time. He's now ready to answer questions from the atproto ecosystem, starting with a crowd favorite: when are we getting an edit button?! (Turns out, it's complicated.)

From there, Toni dove into strategy on everything from moderation to AI. The biggest set of questions was centered on how to build sustainability around an open protocol and how to make that easier for everyone from creators to builders.
