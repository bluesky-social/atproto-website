Planning is underway for the next Atmosphere Conf and the organizers — [Boris Mann](https://bsky.app/profile/bmann.ca), [Guido Jansen](https://bsky.app/profile/gui.do), and [Ms. Boba](https://bsky.app/profile/essentialrandom.bsky.social) — joined the livestream to announce the location (beautiful Amsterdam), dates (April 29–May 2), and the new website at [atmosphereconf.org](https://atmosphereconf.org). They also had news to share about a new organization that's going to be overseeing the conference and other community governance.
