TK intro.

- Spaces use the same two layers as public atproto: infrastructure takedowns, and [labels](/guides/labels) as an annotation layer on top.
- Every service still has to moderate what it hosts and serves.
- What changes is how labels are distributed, plus one more tool: space authorities can remove accounts from a space.

## Labels are records

TK

- Labels on space data are `com.atproto.label.record` records, written into the **same space** as the content they label. Labels that point across space boundaries are ignored.
- They aren't individually signed like public labels. The space repo they're written into authenticates them.
- No `src`, `sig`, `ver`, or `neg` fields; compare with the [public label object](/guides/labels).
- Indexers sync label records like any other space data, then use the usual `atproto-accept-labelers` header to decide which labels to show. The header doesn't go away.
- Label records are for space data only, for now. Public data keeps using signed labels.

TK (open questions, maybe as a Note):

- Labels on whole accounts within a space: not yet specified; space types can define their own behavior for now.
- Labeler declarations: no universal declaration type; apps may define their own.
- Negation by deleting a label record: implied, not stated in the core proposal.

## Who labels a space

TK

- The space authority is always in the space's writer set, so it can always publish labels. It's the default labeler for its space.
- Any other labeler has to be admitted like any other reader (it needs a space credential) and be in the writer set for its labels to sync.
- So a moderation service can't see or label a space it hasn't been admitted to. In practice, the community running a space, or a labeler it chooses, does the labeling.
- Labelers should not publish regular public labels (via `subscribeLabels`) about records in non-public spaces, since that leaks metadata.

## Takedowns and removing members

TK

- By convention, a space authority's `!takedown` label removes content from its space, and syncing services and apps should respect it.
- Space authorities can also remove accounts: `com.atproto.memberspace.removeMembers` and `com.atproto.space.removeWriter`. For restricted spaces this removes read and write access.
- PDS: an account takedown applies to all of that account's space repos; blob takedowns work the same for space blobs.
- AppView: account, space, and individual record takedowns.
- Deactivation and deletion work the same as for public data. If a space authority is taken down or deactivated, its spaces are suspended, not deleted.

## Reporting

TK

- No universal reporting interface for spaces in the protocol. Apps and group specs define their own.
