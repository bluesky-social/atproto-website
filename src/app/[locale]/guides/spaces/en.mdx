## Spaces are small atproto networks

TK: accounts, PDSes, repos, and records, and how a space has the same shape.

<SpacesNetworkDiagram />

## One record, one space

TK: spaces aren't ACLs on records. Each record is in exactly one space; overlapping audiences mean separate spaces with separate records.

<SpacesOneRecordDiagram />

## Space data is atmospheric

TK: space records use Lexicons like any other records, so any app with access can build its own view of them.

<SpacesAppsDiagram />

## Spaces control access, apps control permissions

TK: a space authority is just an account, and decides who's in; roles and finer-grained permissions are up to the app.

<SpacesAccessDiagram />

## Moderation in spaces

TK: labels are records written into the same space as what they label, and synced like any other record; the space authority is the default labeler. Apps apply the labels they accept. See [Moderating spaces](/guides/moderating-spaces).

<SpacesLabelsDiagram />

## Syncing a space

TK: sync without a relay. A writer's PDS notifies the space host (the authority's PDS); the space host notifies sync services; they fetch the record from the writer's PDS with a space credential.

<SpacesNotifyDiagram />

TK: jetstream-spaces does this for every space your app follows, and gives your app one stream.

<SpaceJoinDiagram />
