Reading and writing space records works similarly to public records. The reference `lex` [TypeScript SDK](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/tree/main/packages/lex/lex) provides another package called `lex-space` for working with space data:

<Install />

Records in a space are ordinary atproto records, defined by Lexicons, read and written with the same helpers as [public data](/guides/reading-data). The differences are in where they live and who can read them.

## Creating a space

Spaces have to be created before being written to. Writing to a space that doesn't exist fails with `SpaceNotFound`.

The identity who creates the space becomes the space authority. To create a space, call `createSpace()` with an authenticated client and a [Lexicon for that Space](#space-types):

<CreateSpace />

You can also assign an access policy to a space at creation time. There are three defaults:
- `personalspace`: only the space authority has access.
- `memberspace`: the authority and any other invited users have access.
- `managedspace`: access is delegated to an application which handles permissions.

## Opening a space

Once a Space has been created, call `openSpace()` on it:

<OpenSpace />

This performs credential management as needed in the background. A [space credential](/guides/spaces#syncing-a-space) is fetched at runtime, then cached and renewed.

## Writing records

`create`, `put`, and `delete` operations on space records work just like they do on [public records](/guides/writing-data). Writes always go to the user's own repository, in the space.

<PutDeleteRecord />

**Blobs** are uploaded as normal, then referenced from a space record.

## Space types

A space **type** is a [Lexicon](/guides/lexicon) with `type: "space"`, listing the record collections the space is meant to hold. A required `key` property controls the space key; `literal:self` means one space of this type per account, like a profile record:

<SpaceTypeLexicon />

You can install and build space types like any other Lexicon ([Developing with Lexicons](/guides/installing-lexicons)).

You can also list a user's spaces by `spaceType`:

<ListSpaces />

## App Permissions

**Apps** are granted access to spaces with `space:` OAuth scopes, by space type and authority. See [Permission Requests](/guides/permission-requests):

<Scopes />

Creating a space needs `manage=create`.

`authority=self` refers to a user's own spaces. `authority=*` covers spaces where the user is a member.

`read` reads every repo in a space; `read_self` reads only the user's own.

For every `space:` parameter, and how to include space permissions in a permission set, see [Space permissions](/guides/permission-requests#space-permissions).

## Addressing Space Records

Space record URIs are constructed slightly differently from public records. They begin with `at:space/` and include the space authority and space type:

`at:space/{authority}/{spaceType}/{skey}/{author}/{collection}/{rkey}`.
